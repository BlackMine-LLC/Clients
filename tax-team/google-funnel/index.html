Questions

The things founders ask us first.

How is this different from my CPA?
Most CPAs file after the year closes — they record what already happened. We work ahead of it. We look at your business and personal finances as one system, all year, so decisions get made with a plan instead of a deadline.
Why add you to my bookkeeper and CPA?
Because right now no one is looking at the whole picture. A bookkeeper tracks the past, a once-a-year CPA files it, and the strategy in between falls to you. We connect the pieces so the whole thing points in one direction.
What does it cost?
It depends on the complexity of your business and personal situation — that's exactly what the strategy call is for. There's no cost and no obligation to have that first conversation.
Do you handle business and personal together?
Yes. That's the point. One team handles both returns and the planning across them, so nothing falls through the gap between "the business" and "you."
What happens on the call?
A 30-minute conversation about where the business is, what's on your mind, and where a proactive plan could help. No pitch — just clarity on whether we're the right fit.
