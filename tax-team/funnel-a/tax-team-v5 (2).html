If we can't identify meaningful tax savings opportunities, you won't pay for a strategy that doesn't deliver. We stand behind what we do — and our 15% participation model means we have every incentive to find real results, not just charge a fee.

Our strategies deliver the most value for business owners and high-income earners at $400K+ annually. Below that threshold, the savings potential usually doesn't justify the engagement. We'd rather tell you that upfront than take on a client who won't see the full return.
