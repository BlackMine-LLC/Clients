5 Patterns That Repeat Across Long-Runners

Pulled from ads live 200+ days. Each pattern includes example hooks and a one-line "for Aileen" application.

1

Niche-Down Hooks

Open with the exact ICP — not a generic intro.

  • Karlton Dennis: "For High-Income Professionals & Business Owners Paying Over $100k/Year In Taxes"
  • Kyle Slagle: "❗❗ Attention Physicians! ❗❗" (380 days live)
  • EthicsPro: "REIs Who Paid Over $100k in Taxes Last Year" (300 days live)
  • Natalie Dawson: "If you own an e-commerce brand… doing over $1M per year"

For Aileen: Open every ad by naming Aileen's exact buyer — income tier, role, or revenue band. "For business owners paying over $X in taxes" beats "tax season is here" every time.

2

Reverse-Risk Guarantees

Make a specific dollar promise + take the risk off the prospect.

  • EthicsPro: "Give me 10 minutes and we will find at least $50,000 in tax savings... Guaranteed. If I can't? I'll personally cut you a check for $100." (300 days live)
  • Ed Lloyd: "Save up to 90% on your taxes with a customized tax plan."

For Aileen: Bake a specific $-or-time guarantee into one variant. "Find $X in savings or [what you'll do]" — concrete number always beats vague promise.

3

Curiosity-Gap Listicles

A list teaser + DM/Subscribe CTA. The lowest-friction first ad to test.

  • Codie Sanchez: "3 businesses I would never buy… Let me tell you why" (620+ days, IG, Send Message CTA)
  • Karlton/Uplevel: "💡 Three levers drive real tax savings."

For Aileen: Run something like "3 deductions every [ICP] is missing" or "3 quarterly mistakes I see every year" with a Send-Message or Subscribe CTA. No offer, no pricing, no urgency. Pure curiosity → DM.

4

Pain-Point Opener with Sensory Detail

Specific moments > abstract overwhelm.

  • Natalie Dawson: "You built a beautiful business. And somehow you're still the one doing everything. Covering shifts. Chasing down no-shows. Training the same person for the third time."
  • Natalie Dawson: "You've built something great. But now the same effort is producing half the results. You're constantly putting out fires." (265 days live)

For Aileen: Replace abstractions ("you're overwhelmed") with concrete moments ("watching another quarterly estimate hit your account on a Friday afternoon"). Aileen's tonal lane.

5

Reframe Hook (against a competing identity)

Give the buyer a new mental model to opt into.

  • Karlton Dennis: "CPAs focus on filing returns, not saving you money. That's why you need a Tax Strategist instead."
  • Codie Sanchez: "Don't Build — Buy instead."
  • Natalie Dawson: "Scaling isn't about working harder. It's about plugging into the right playbook."
  • Mia Taylor: "Same credentials as IRS agents… Except I'm on YOUR side."

For Aileen: Pick the closest competing identity — generic CPA, DIY tax software, the H&R Block office, the bookkeeper-who-doesn't-strategize — and reframe against it.
