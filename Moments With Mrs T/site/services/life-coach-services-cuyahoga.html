What is Life Coaching?

Life coaching is not therapy — it's a forward-focused, action-based partnership. I help you create a plan for your personal or emotional goals, and stick to it with accountability and kindness.

Through coaching, you can:

  • Set meaningful goals and follow through
  • Heal emotional blocks that keep you stuck
  • Build confidence and self-worth
  • Create peace after chaos or change
  • Get practical steps to make life feel manageable again

I work with adults, parents, caregivers, and teens (16+) in one-on-one or group formats — and I tailor each plan to your specific needs.

Why Life Coaching Matters in Cuyahoga County

Living in Cuyahoga, the emotional toll of daily life is real. I see it in the people I coach — the long winters that drain motivation, the school and work stress that leaves families disconnected, and the nonstop social media pressure that eats away at self-worth.

You’re not lazy, broken, or too late — you’re just exhausted. And with the right tools, support, and space to breathe, you can rebuild your confidence and clarity.

That’s why I offer both in-person and online life coaching — so support is always available, no matter the season, schedule, or situation.
