Mrs. T's Chair

Mrs. T's Chair isn't just a name — it's a symbol of stillness, safety, and support. It's where the overwhelmed parent finally exhales. It's where the teen who's been misunderstood finds their voice. It's where organizations send those they care about most — because they know they'll be met with warmth, wisdom, and real help.

About

About Moments With Mrs. T

Healing and moving forward should be a supported, empowering journey.

I'm Tieshia Henry, a certified life coach with a heart for service. I founded Moments With Mrs. T in 2023 to offer wraparound services that help you acknowledge, process, and triumph over life's challenges.

My approach blends case management, coping strategies, and personalized care, so you never have to walk the path alone. I'm proudly based in Lorain County and serve the surrounding Cuyahoga areas with dedication and compassion.

FAQs

Questions I hear often

Here to answer your worries and put your mind at ease.

What makes Moments With Mrs. T different from other life coaching services?

My compassionate, lived-experience approach combined with certified expertise creates a genuinely nurturing environment for transformation.

How long are your programs?

Programs range from 3 to 12 months, tailored to your personal growth needs.

Do you offer group sessions?

Yes! I provide both one-on-one and group workshop sessions for coping skills and life strategy building.

Can life coaching sessions be virtual?

Yes, I offer both in-person and virtual coaching sessions for your convenience.

What ages do you serve?

I work with children starting in the 4th grade up to adults seeking guidance, healing, and empowerment.

In crisis or thinking about harming yourself? Coaching isn't emergency care. Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) any time, or dial 911.
