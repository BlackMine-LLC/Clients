Let's talk
What makes Moments With Mrs. T different from other life coaching services?

A blend of genuine empathy and professional certification. I bring lived experience to every session, which creates a supportive environment for real personal growth and transformation.

How long are your programs?

Programs range from 3 to 12 months, designed around your goals, challenges, and strengths.

Can coaching help with seasonal stress or winter depression?

Absolutely. Seasonal Affective Disorder (SAD) is very real in Ohio. The long, dark winters and gray skies in Cuyahoga and Lorain can deeply affect your mood. I help you build coping strategies that keep you grounded, energized, and emotionally balanced — no matter the season.

Family Coaching also helps reduce seasonal stress at home.

What if I don't know where to start?

That's perfectly OK. These services are built for people who feel overwhelmed, unsure, or just need a starting point. Begin by scheduling a free consultation and I'll help guide your path forward — without pressure or judgment.

Do you take insurance?

I don't currently accept insurance, but rates are affordable and flexible, with discounts for long-term commitments and bundled sessions.

Is this service available in person or online?

Both! I serve clients across Cuyahoga, Lorain, and Strongsville in person, and I also offer secure virtual sessions — especially helpful during tough Ohio winters when travel is hard.

Do you offer group sessions or family-based coaching?

Yes! Many clients find group coaching comforting and relatable. Family sessions are also available for improving communication, rebuilding trust, or healing after difficult times.

See Family Coaching and Coping Skills & Workshops to learn more.

What ages do you serve? Do you help teens?

I work with children starting in the 4th grade, teens, and adults — especially those navigating big changes, family conflict, or emotional overwhelm. Sessions are always adapted to fit the age and needs of the individual.

Is this therapy?

No. Life coaching and case management are forward-focused and action-based. They're a great fit alongside clinical care, but they aren't a replacement for it. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or dial 911.

Are results guaranteed?

Every journey is unique. What I can guarantee is my commitment to your progress, honest feedback, and professional support every step of the way. Transformation takes time, and I'm with you for the long haul.

In crisis or thinking about harming yourself? Coaching isn't emergency care. Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) any time, or dial 911.
