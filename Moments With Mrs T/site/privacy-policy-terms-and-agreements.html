Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Moments With Mrs. T (“we,” “us”), a life coaching and case management practice founded by Tieshia Henry and serving Lorain County, Cuyahoga County, and virtual clients, handles information when you visit this website or contact us.

1. Information you give us

When you request a consultation or contact us, we collect what you choose to share: your name, phone number, email address, the best time to reach you, the service you're interested in, any message you write, and whether you opted in to appointment text messages.

Please don't include detailed medical or mental-health information in the website form. You can share that privately during your consultation.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Hosting logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel) records basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure, and prevent abuse.
  • Analytics — only with your permission. If you choose “Allow analytics,” we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site: pages viewed, time on page, clicks, scrolling, device and browser type, and approximate location. Clarity may create session replays and heatmaps. Form fields are masked and never recorded. If you decline, Clarity does not load. See Cookies & analytics.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and schedule, confirm, or reschedule appointments
  • To send reminders or updates about your sessions
  • To answer questions you send us
  • To keep the website secure and, if you allow analytics, to improve it

We do not sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or use it to send marketing messages.

4. Who helps us run the site

We share information only with service providers that help us operate, and only as needed:

  • Vercel — website hosting and security.
  • FormSubmit — delivers your consultation request from the website to our email inbox.
  • Google — our email inbox (Gmail). Google Maps loads only if you click “Show map.”
  • Microsoft Clarity — website analytics, only if you allow it.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect someone's safety in an emergency.

5. How long we keep it

We keep consultation requests and contact details only as long as needed to respond and to provide services, and we delete them when you ask (unless we're required to keep them). Clarity keeps analytics data according to Microsoft's retention policy (recordings for about 30 days, aggregated data for up to about 13 months).

6. How we protect it

The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS with modern security settings, access to your information is limited to Moments With Mrs. T, and we never sell or rent it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children and teens

We work with children (4th grade and up), teens, and families, but services for anyone under 18 are arranged by a parent or legal guardian. This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information through this site, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Ask to see, correct, or delete the information we have about you.
  • Stop appointment texts any time by replying STOP, or contact us.
  • Change your analytics choice any time with the Privacy choices link in the footer.
  • We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal as a request to opt out of analytics.

To make a request, email momentwithmrst@gmail.com or call (330) 249-1042. We'll respond within 30 days.

Cookies & analytics

This site uses no advertising cookies. We store your privacy choice in your browser so we don't ask again. If, and only if, you choose “Allow analytics,” Microsoft Clarity sets these first-party cookies:

  • _clck — keeps a random Clarity user ID (up to 1 year)
  • _clsk — connects page views into one session (1 day)

Microsoft may also use its own cookies when processing Clarity data. Learn more in the Microsoft Privacy Statement and the Clarity cookie list.

Text messages (SMS)

Texting is optional. If you check the box on our booking form, you agree to receive text messages from Moments With Mrs. T only about your appointments (confirmations, reminders, and rescheduling). Consent is not required to book.

  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.
  • We do not send promotional or marketing texts.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be noted on this page.
