Music Hits So Different Now

A letter from Tieshia Henry to her husband, J · 3 min read

J ♥️💔♥️

Well, Bae, yesterday I was riding home listening to some music, and JASONNNNN, I came to a realization! 😂❤️

MUSIC HITS SO DIFFERENT NOW!

J, I realized I still couldn’t listen to certain love songs. Songs like Never Make a Promise and all those beautiful R&B songs about loving somebody, being faithful, and spending your life together.

Bae, THAT WAS YOU! THAT WAS US! 🥹❤️

You did everything you said you were going to do, and then you went ABOVE AND BEYOND!

And that’s why those songs were so hard for me to listen to.

But J, here’s the crazy part!

I WAS LISTENING TO ALL THOSE CHEATING SONGS JUST FINE! 😂😂

SIRRRRR! Mr. Bigg came on, and then here came Mary J. Blige singing Not Gon’ Cry!

BABYYYYY! 😂

I was riding down the road listening to all these songs about cheating, lying, crying, and trying to figure out where somebody’s man was!

And I was sitting there thinking, “LORD, I’M SO GRATEFUL I NEVER HAD TO GO THROUGH ALL THAT!” 🙌🏽❤️

All that crying, snotting, hoping, wishing, wondering where your man was, and trying to figure out what he was doing?

BABY, THAT WAS TOO MUCH! WE DON’T GET PAID FOR ALL THAT! 😂😂

JASONNNNN! You knew I loved my blues and old-school R&B, but listening to those songs yesterday was something else!

It was funny because I had listened to that same music when you were here, and I never really thought too deeply about the words.

But yesterday, I heard everything differently.

BECAUSE J, I NEVER HAD TO LIVE THROUGH HALF THE THINGS THOSE PEOPLE WERE SINGING ABOUT!

And for that, I thanked God! 🙌🏽❤️

You left an imprint on my heart and a blueprint of what love, commitment, friendship, and partnership looked like for almost 30 years.

ALMOST THIRTY YEARS, J!

And Bae, I just couldn’t imagine anybody coming along and trumping that.

Not because I was looking for somebody to compare to you.

SIRRRRR, I WAS MINDING MY OWN BUSINESS! 😂

I was simply riding home, thinking about raising our babies, keeping my mind together, handling everyday life, and learning how to navigate this journey without you physically here.

But as I listened to those songs, I realized all over again just how blessed I had been.

We weren’t perfect. We didn’t agree on everything. We had our moments, like everybody else.

But I NEVER HAD TO SIT AROUND WONDERING IF MY HUSBAND LOVED ME OR IF HE WAS GOING TO SHOW UP FOR OUR FAMILY.

You showed up, J.

You loved us, you stood by us, and you gave me a kind of love that I will carry for the rest of my life.

And yesterday, I understood a little more why the love songs hurt worse than the cheating songs.

THE CHEATING SONGS TOLD STORIES I NEVER HAD TO LIVE. THE LOVE SONGS REMINDED ME OF A LIFE I ACTUALLY HAD. 🥺💔

WHEW, BAE!

Now THAT made me think!

I could laugh at all the foolishness in those other songs, but when somebody started singing about loving their person forever, my heart went straight to you.

Because I knew what that kind of love felt like.

I LIVED IT WITH YOU.

And although I missed you more than words could explain, I found myself thanking God for the love He allowed me to experience.

“LORD, THANK YOU FOR THE LOVE YOU ALLOWED ME TO EXPERIENCE.” 🙌🏽❤️

J, you really did leave some mighty big shoes behind.

And I don’t take one moment of our love for granted.

By the time I finished listening to Mr. Bigg and Mary J., all I could think was, “THESE FOLKS WERE GOING THROUGH IT, AND I WAS JUST TRYING TO GET HOME!” 😂😂❤️

Love you and miss you on purpose, my Sugga Bear. ♥️💔♥️

Your Sugga Lump.
Your Wife.
Your Bestie.

CYLLYB.
