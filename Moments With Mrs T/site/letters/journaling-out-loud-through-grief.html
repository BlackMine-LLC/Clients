Journaling Out Loud

A letter from Tieshia Henry to her husband, Jay · 4 min read

J ♥️💔♥️

Well, Bae, THINGS ARE HAPPENING! 🥹❤️🙌🏽

JASONNNNN! I’ve been speaking with people who are cheering on our book, encouraging me to continue writing, and reminding me that these daily letters to you are touching hearts.

And J, I am SO grateful!

You know, I’ve come to realize that writing to you every day is something I don’t have to do, but it’s something I WANT to do.

And I believe it will always hold a special place in my heart.

Bae, you’ve been resting in the arms of the Lord for almost five months now, and here I am, still talking to you about everyday life.

I’M JUST JOURNALING OUT LOUD, J! ❤️

That’s really what these letters are.

My thoughts, my feelings, our memories, the funny things our boys do, and those moments when grief shows up without warning.

I understand you’re not physically here to answer me, but that doesn’t mean I have to keep everything bottled up inside.

WHEN MY HEART HAS SOMETHING TO SAY, I NEED TO GIVE IT SOMEWHERE TO GO.

And for me, that’s writing.

J, isn’t it something how God works?

For years, even before Moments With Mrs. T became what it is today, I encouraged students to express what they were feeling.

Sometimes through conversations, sometimes through writing, and sometimes through drawing.

I wanted them to understand that their feelings mattered and that they didn’t have to carry everything silently.

And now, Bae, here I am practicing the very things I’ve spent years encouraging others to do!

SIRRRRR! LOOK AT GOD! 🙌🏽❤️

Even in my coaching sessions today, I talk about the importance of journaling.

It doesn’t have to be some expensive, beautiful journal.

BABY, GET YOU A SPIRAL NOTEBOOK, A PIECE OF PAPER, OR EVEN THE BACK OF SOMETHING YOU ALREADY HAVE! 😂❤️

Just start writing.

Because sometimes putting those feelings into words can bring a little relief and help us understand what’s happening inside.

And J, now that I’m experiencing grief from this side, I understand something even deeper.

GRIEF IS A WHOLE NOTEBOOK OF FEELINGS THAT WORDS CAN BARELY CONTAIN! 🥺💔

I can write about what I’m experiencing, but sometimes trying to explain it out loud is another story.

So I write about what I’m feeling in the moment.

Not what somebody thinks I should be feeling.

Not what grief is supposed to look like.

JUST WHERE I AM, RIGHT NOW.

And Bae, I don’t like saying I’m letting it go.

Because I’m not letting YOU go.

I’m not letting our memories go.

I’m not letting the love we shared go.

I’M SIMPLY OPENING MY HEART AND RELEASING SOME OF THE WEIGHT I’VE BEEN CARRYING.

There’s a difference, J.

I’m learning how to process through grief without allowing it to keep everything inside me bound up.

And I’m grateful that God continues to give me the strength and the words to do that.

What really touches me is knowing that these letters might be helping somebody else.

Maybe somebody reads one and realizes they’re not alone.

Maybe somebody finds the courage to talk about their own grief.

Maybe somebody finally picks up a pen and begins writing about what they’ve been carrying.

AND J, IF ONE PERSON FINDS A LITTLE RELIEF THROUGH SOMETHING I’VE SHARED, I’M GRATEFUL! ❤️

Because this was never about having the perfect words.

It’s about being transparent, loving you out loud, and allowing others to see that grief and gratitude can exist together.

And Bae, I’m so thankful that God has allowed me to continue speaking about US.

Our love.

Our family.

Our memories.

And this life I’m learning to navigate without you physically here.

MOMENTS WITH MRS. T HAS ALWAYS BEEN ABOUT HELPING PEOPLE FIND THEIR VOICE.

And now, through these letters, I’m continuing to find mine in a season I never wanted to experience.

J, I don’t know how many people these words will reach or what God has planned for them.

But I know one thing.

I’M GOING TO KEEP WRITING. KEEP PROCESSING. KEEP LOVING. AND KEEP TRUSTING GOD! 🙌🏽❤️

Because He didn’t have to give me this outlet, this gift, or the opportunity to encourage others through our story.

But He did.

And for that, I AM SO GRATEFUL!

Bae, I miss you terribly. I wish I could tell you all these things while looking into your eyes instead of writing them down.

But until then, I’ll keep journaling out loud.

ONE DAY, ONE MEMORY, ONE MOMENT AT A TIME.

And I’ll keep moving forward, in Jesus’ name. 🙏🏽❤️

Love you and miss you on purpose, my Sugga Bear. ♥️💔♥️

Your Sugga Lump.
Your Wife.
Your Bestie.

CYLLYB.
