Always Part of My Story

A letter from Tieshia Henry to her husband, J · 3 min read

J ♥️💔♥️

BAEEEEEE! ❤️🥹 Yesterday was another one of those days when I enjoyed myself, but MY GOD, I wished you were there!

Yesterday, I attended an event celebrating the scholarship fund in honor of the late Pastor Lindsay. It was such a beautiful occasion, Bae! So many people showed me love, and I was truly grateful.

BUT J, THE ONLY THING MISSING WAS YOU! 💔

And SIRRRRR, let me tell you how my evening started! 😂

I got my hair done, got dressed, and was trying to make sure everything was together. I still needed a few things for the basket bag I was putting together for the event.

So I stopped at the store around 6:34 .

BAE, THE EVENT STARTED AT 7:00! 😂

The cashier helped me find what I needed, and somehow we ended up talking.

And you already know what happened next!

MOMENTS WITH MRS. T SHOWED UP RIGHT THERE IN THAT STORE! 🙌🏽❤️

Bae, I found myself encouraging her, speaking life, and sharing what God placed on my heart.

And before I knew it, IT WAS 7:11!

JASONNNNN! I WAS STILL IN THE STORE! 😂

Now, normally I would’ve been frustrated because I was late. But when I thought about it afterward, I couldn’t even be upset.

BECAUSE SOMETIMES YOUR PURPOSE DOESN’T OPERATE ON YOUR PERSONAL SCHEDULE!

I want Moments With Mrs. T to operate like a normal business, with appointments, sessions, and schedules.

But J, when God places somebody in your path who needs encouragement, sometimes you have to be willing to stop and be available.

GOD WOULDN’T HAVE PLACED ME THERE IF HE DIDN’T HAVE A PURPOSE FOR ME BEING THERE.

And Bae, that made me think.

Maybe part of my purpose is simply being available whenever God leads me to encourage somebody, no matter where I am.

SO I’M GOING TO KEEP ON KEEPING ON, J! IN JESUS’ NAME! 🙌🏽❤️

Now, let’s get back to this event!

SIRRRRR! You would’ve been FUSSING because we were you were hungry! 😂

JASONNNNN! I got there right around the ending of the appetizers, and you would’ve had ALL THE QUESTIONS!

But I still enjoyed a good meal and had a wonderful time.

And Bae, that’s what I missed most.

Having you there to laugh with, talk to, and enjoy those moments together.

Even while getting ready, I still thought about you.

WHAT WOULD J THINK ABOUT THIS HAIRSTYLE? THIS OUTFIT? THESE NAILS? ❤️

I still think about your reaction to the little things.

I still imagine what you would’ve said and what we would’ve laughed about afterward.

Because for almost 30 years, you were part of all those moments.

I CAN HAVE A WONDERFUL TIME AND STILL WISH MY HUSBAND WAS STANDING BESIDE ME.

And that’s exactly how yesterday felt.

I’m grateful for the people who continue to show me love. I’m grateful for every opportunity God gives me to encourage somebody. And I’m grateful that I can still get dressed up, go somewhere, laugh, and enjoy life.

BUT BAE, I WILL ALWAYS WISH YOU WERE HERE TO EXPERIENCE IT WITH ME. 🥹💔

I miss your questions, your fussing, your excitement, and your unique way of making every outing OUR outing.

J, please keep watching over us.

I’m going to keep walking in my purpose, loving our babies, and trusting God with this journey.

BECAUSE YOU MAY NOT BE IN THE PICTURE PHYSICALLY, BUT YOU WILL ALWAYS BE PART OF MY STORY. ❤️

Love you and miss you on purpose, my Sugga Bear. ♥️💔♥️

Your Sugga Lump.
Your Wife.
Your Bestie.

CYLLYB.
