The Battle Is the Lord’s

A letter from Tieshia Henry to her husband, J · 4 min read

J ♥️💔♥️

Bae😭💔yesterday morning, I was on my way to work when Yolanda Adams came on the radio singing The Battle Is the Lord’s.

J, THAT WAS YOUR SONG!

The song you always wanted me to sing to you through all those hospital visits, procedures, and difficult seasons we faced together.

And Bae, when that song started playing, IT TOOK ME ALL THE WAY OUT!

I wasn’t just crying. I WAS SOBBING, J! SOBBING! 😭

And the closer I got to work, the harder those tears fell.

JASONNNNN, I NEEDED THOSE TEARS TO GET THEMSELVES TOGETHER!

Because I couldn’t walk through those doors looking the way I was looking!

But Bae, I couldn’t stop.

My heart was so sad, yet somehow there was joy and gratitude in there, too.

TWO COMPLETELY DIFFERENT FEELINGS WERE LIVING IN THE SAME HEART AT THE SAME TIME.

Sad because I missed you terribly.

Grateful because of the love we shared, the life we built, and the God we served together.

And J, that song resonated in my spirit like never before.

THIS BATTLE WAS NOT MINE. IT BELONGED TO THE LORD! 🙌🏽💔

But MY GOD, I hated that I was the one having to walk through that part of our journey without you.

I DID! I DID! I DID!

We went through so much together, Bae. All those hospital stays, the uncertainty, the prayers, the waiting, and the moments when we didn’t know what tomorrow would bring.

But through it all, WE HAD EACH OTHER!

We never chose to go through life’s battles separately.

It was always US.

And if somebody had asked me, J, I would’ve chosen to continue facing every battle with you right beside me.

Because that’s how we did life.

TOGETHER!

And there I was, trying to understand a journey I never would’ve chosen for myself.

Lord Jesus, please! 😭🙏🏽

That was all I could say yesterday morning.

LORD, PLEASE!

I asked Him to help me, strengthen me, and continue watching over me and our babies.

And J, I found myself asking God for something else.

LORD, PLEASE SHOW ME A SIGN THAT YOU’RE WATCHING OVER US.

I knew what I believed. I knew where my faith stood. I knew God had continued to carry us.

BUT LORD, SOMETIMES I JUST NEEDED THAT EXTRA REASSURANCE!

Not because I didn’t believe.

Not because I didn’t trust Him.

But because I was a wife missing her husband, a mother raising our babies, and a woman trying to find her way through a reality she never wanted.

AND SOMETIMES, EVEN WHEN YOU KNOW, YOU STILL NEED COMFORT. 🥺❤️

Bae, wasn’t that something?

That song had always been one you wanted ME to sing to encourage YOU through your battles.

And yesterday, that same song came through my radio when I needed encouragement for mine.

MY GOD, THAT SONG ATE ME UP YESTERDAY MORNING!

I couldn’t explain the tears. I couldn’t stop them. I could only feel everything that was happening in my heart.

The sadness. The love. The gratitude. The memories. The longing.

ALL OF IT, J. ALL OF IT!

And even through those tears, I thanked God.

Because I knew what it felt like to love you, to be loved by you, and to walk beside you for almost 30 years.

I wouldn’t have traded that love for anything.

I just wished we had been given more time.

So yesterday, I placed what I couldn’t carry into God’s hands.

LORD, I ASKED YOU TO KEEP WATCHING OVER US, KEEP OUR BABIES COVERED, AND HELP ME THROUGH THOSE MOMENTS WHEN MY HEART FELT LIKE IT COULDN’T TAKE ANYMORE.

J, I thought about how much I loved you and how deeply I missed you.

And Bae, I knew there were feelings in my heart that I would probably never be able to fully explain.

THAT BATTLE WAS THE LORD’S, AND YESTERDAY I TRUSTED HIM TO CARRY ME THROUGH IT, EVEN WITH TEARS RUNNING DOWN MY FACE. 🙏🏽♥️💔

Love you and miss you on purpose, my Sugga Bear.

Your Sugga Lump.
Your Wife.
Your Bestie.

CYLLYB.
